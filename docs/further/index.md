# Phase 10 · Signals & Dynamics

**Goal:** two topics that connect everything you've learned to physics, engineering and modern ML: **differential equations** (how systems evolve over time) and **Fourier analysis** (how signals decompose into frequencies).

**Why it matters:** physics is written in differential equations. Gradient descent is a discretized differential equation, and so are diffusion models, neural ODEs and state-space sequence models. The Fourier transform underlies signal processing, the FFT, convolutions, positional encodings and much of physics.

**What you'll see:** the ideas from earlier phases working together. Exponentials and Euler's formula (Phase 1), Taylor series (Phase 2), eigenvalues (Phase 3) and orthogonal projection (Phase 3) are exactly the tools both topics need.

**Time:** about 8–10 hours.
