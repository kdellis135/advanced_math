# Phase 10 · Signals & Dynamics

**Goal:** two topics that connect everything you've learned to physics, engineering and modern ML:

| Topic | Studies |
|---|---|
| **Differential equations** | how systems evolve over time |
| **Fourier analysis** | how signals decompose into frequencies |

**Why it matters:**

- Physics is written in differential equations.
- Gradient descent is a discretized differential equation, and so are diffusion models, neural ODEs and state-space sequence models.
- The Fourier transform underlies signal processing, the FFT, convolutions, positional encodings and much of physics.

**What you'll see:** the ideas from earlier phases working together. Both topics need exactly these tools:

- exponentials and Euler's formula (Phase 1);
- Taylor series (Phase 2);
- eigenvalues and orthogonal projection (Phase 3).

**Time:** about 8–10 hours.
